GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Does the AI write lyrics?+

No. Lyrics and timings come from the provider and the shared engine. The optional backend can only suggest mood, section, palette and intensity — visual decisions only.

Does AUTO switch on every beat?+

No. It holds a look briefly before changing, and needs a real drop or a longer hold to switch. That hysteresis is what keeps the display calm.

Is my music uploaded anywhere?+

No. BPM, beat, bass and energy are derived locally from the Android Visualizer bridge — or, here on the web, from the Web Audio API on a file you choose.

Does this add a second player?+

No. All eight lyrics modes and the visualizer read the single Media3/ExoPlayer clock. One queue, one position, one source of truth.
