Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

AURA MUSIC is built around one principle: your listening stays yours. This policy covers the Android app and this website. It explains what is stored on your device, what is processed in your browser, and the small number of third parties involved only if you choose to use them.

1. What stays on your device

In the app, playlists, favourites, the playback queue and history, lyrics and offsets, your cinematic theme choice and the optional SMART learning profile are kept in a local Room database and DataStore preferences. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of it. There is no account sign-in and no profile synchronised to a server owned by this project.

2. Audio and visual analysis

BPM, beat, bass, mid, treble, drop, intensity and energy are computed on the device from Android's Visualizer API (with your RECORD_AUDIO permission) or, on this website, from the Web Audio API using a file you pick or your microphone. No audio is uploaded, recorded to a file, or sent to any server. Denying the permission only disables the reactive visuals; the rest of the app keeps working.

3. Lyrics and timestamps

Lyrics and their timings come from the provider you use and the shared lyrics engine. The optional intelligence backend can suggest mood, section, palette and intensity to steer visuals — it never writes a lyric or a timestamp. The website's style lab is a local demonstration with fixed sample words; it does not fetch or store your music.

4. The optional Groq intelligence backend

This is entirely opt-in and self-hosted by you. If you never configure a backend, nothing is sent. If you do, the payload contains only:

  • theme colour dictionary and theme_name
  • features: bass, beat, energy, tempo
  • position_ms, duration_ms, playing
  • lyrics, lyrics_visible and word timings

Audio is never part of this payload. GROQ_API_KEY lives only on that backend environment; it is never embedded in the app or this site. The backend calls Groq to produce visual steering only.

There are no advertisements, advertising identifiers, attribution SDKs, payment flows, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the shipping app or on this site. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are disabled in the delivered build.

6. Cookies

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. A cookie banner is therefore not required. Your browser may cache static files normally.

7. Big Screen / receiver

Big Screen output uses Android display APIs or a receiver you pair yourself with a code. Pairing state and receiver name stay in local app state. A generic media-routing service, if your device offers one, routes media playback only — it is not a channel for AURA MUSIC's visual state and is not controlled by this project.

8. No sale of data

AURA MUSIC does not sell, trade or rent personal information and does not use your listening data for marketing.

9. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke microphone permission to disable audio analysis.
  • Leave the intelligence backend unconfigured to keep all processing local.
  • Choose MANUAL instead of SMART cinematic mode.
  • Unpair a Big Screen receiver at any time.
  • Uninstall the app or clear site data to remove local information.

10. Children

AURA MUSIC is not directed at children under 13, and no personal information is knowingly collected from them.

11. Changes

This policy may change as the project evolves. The date at the top always indicates the current version.
